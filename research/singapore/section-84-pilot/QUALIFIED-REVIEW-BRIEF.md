## Proposed positive scope and exclusions

The proposed future **non-executable** review covers only Penal Code 1871 s 84's post-2019 and post-2022 statutory structure, its temporal selection, Evidence Act 1893 s 107's burden relationship, and CPC procedure as a *separate boundary*. The [2019 Act, s 27](https://assets.egazette.gov.sg/2019/Legislative%20Supplements/Acts%20Supplement/23.pdf), [2021 Act, s 8](https://assets.egazette.gov.sg/2021/Legislative%20Supplements/Acts%20Supplement/23.pdf), [2019 commencement, cl 2](https://assets.egazette.gov.sg/2019/Legislative%20Supplements/Subsidiary%20Legislation%20Supplement/849.pdf), and [2022 commencement, cl 2](https://assets.egazette.gov.sg/2022/Legislative%20Supplements/Subsidiary%20Legislation%20Supplement/146.pdf) are the key enactment chain. Direct point-in-time SSO PC/EA/CPC access returned 403 on 13 September 2026.

Exclude evidence assessment, medical diagnosis, witness credibility, actual legal proof, intoxication, automatism, diminished responsibility, fitness decisions, custody/release, guilty pleas, guilt, conviction, acquittal, sentencing, and any legal advice or certificate of current law. No court outcome is inferred from a kernel rule result. Section 323 is only a possible later anchor; it is **not** approved here. No executable KernelInput exists for this pilot; this packet is not a valid `ModelBundle-v1`.
