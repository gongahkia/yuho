# Singapore section 84 pilot: legal-review packet

**Status:** questions and acceptance criteria for a future Singapore-qualified criminal-law reviewer, 13 September 2026. No reviewer, signature, approval, authenticated authority or legal conclusion is asserted. This packet is not legal advice. See the [decision](SINGAPORE-SECTION-84-PILOT-DECISION-REPORT.md), [source ledger](SINGAPORE-SECTION-84-SOURCE-LEDGER.md) and [proposition matrix](SINGAPORE-SECTION-84-DOCTRINAL-MATRIX.md).

## Proposed item for review

Review a **source-pinned statutory-structure/representability map** of Penal Code 1871 s 84 as applicable to a **specified hypothetical act date on or after 1 March 2022**. It would enumerate s 84(1)'s common temporal/causal premise and three alternative incapacity limbs, and s 84(2)'s two linked wrongfulness clauses. It would record EA 1893 s 107 and CPC 2010 Part 13 Division 5 as **context requiring separate verification**, not silently treat them as evaluated rules. Review must name the exact point-in-time legislative expression and positive semantic IDs; no single “current” label or URL suffices. The initial bundle may contain a source-linked technical tree for inspection, but it must not produce a claim about an actual person or act.

Explicitly outside positive scope: any real accused, offence charge/count, medical diagnosis, expert-evidence weighting, legal burden discharge, guilt, innocence, conviction, acquittal, fitness to plead, CPC finding/order, confinement, supervision, release, appeal, sentence, other Penal Code general exceptions, intoxication, diminished responsibility, automatism, and legal advice. A later s 323 anchor is conditional, not part of initial review. This packet does not claim to model the whole Penal Code or all possible defences.

## Source checklist and retrieval gate

1. Acquire exact official PC s 84 point-in-time text, the 2019 replacement (Act 15/2019 s 27), the 2021 amendment (Act 23/2021 s 8), each commencement notification, and any later changes affecting the selected act date [S01–S04]. Resolve the SSO 7-Oct/Gazette 8-Oct 2021 publication label discrepancy; it does not itself change commencement. Check whether an amended consolidated expression agrees with as-published Acts. Record artifact digests, media types, extraction process and exact spans separately; a URL or hash alone does not authenticate law.
2. Acquire exact official EA s 107 and surrounding burden provisions for the relevant trial period, including amendments/savings [S05]. Verify the court-presumption language, illustration and defence standard against appropriate appellate authority; the SSO index is insufficient as a final review artifact.
3. Acquire exact CPC 2010 ss 246–256A (and current ss 256B–256C where relevant), Criminal Justice Reform Act 2018 and S 882/2022, Act 5/2024 and S 21/2025, and all applicable transitional text for a **specified proceeding date** [S06–S08, S12–S15]. Do not substitute the text of old ss 251–252 quoted in *Tan Kok Meng* for later law.
4. Read published *Tan Kok Meng* [2021] SGCA 55 [19]–[27] [S16] for its actual act-finding holding and 2015 conduct date; *Roszaidi* [2022] SGCA 75 [87]–[93] [S09] as a post-amendment quotation/comparison, **not** an s 84 holding; *Tan Chor Jin* [2008] SGCA 32 [19]–[24] [S17] for the older s 84/s 85 distinction; *PP v GCK* [2020] SGCA 2 [129]–[133] [S10] for enduring prosecution burden; and *PP v Han John Han* [2007] SGHC 8 [5]–[9] [S18] for the diagnosis/legal-test caution. Obtain PDF versions or confirm HTML conversion where quotations or typography matter. Recheck for later overruling and legislative displacement.
5. Retrieve *Jumaat bin Mohamed Sayed and others v Attorney-General* [2025] SGCA 40 directly if available [S20]. Its eLitigation endpoint returned a maintenance page here; no indexed passage is treated as verified judgment text. Review any relevance of its EA s 107 discussion only after actual access.
6. If proceeding to an anchor, source-review PC ss 319/321/323 together with ss 323A/334 and possible general exceptions for an expressly bounded s 323 model [S04]. Compare s 279 and s 352 before final selection. Do not infer a complete offence package from a single section heading.

Any unresolved answer that determines the meaning of an executable leaf or exception blocks a reviewed executable pilot. A source-access gap is not filled by a corpus `.yh` transcription, search snippet or synthetic scenario.

## Synthetic scenario categories for later **technical** inspection

These categories are questions about the shape of a proposed model, **not** Singapore legal facts or approved fixtures: nature-incapacity route with wrongfulness routes unresolved; moral-incapacity alone versus legal-incapacity alone versus both (post-2022 syntax); complete-control-deprivation route while cognition remains supplied as established; a condition at another time without an act-time causal classification; an unresolved supplied classification; an anchor offence technically satisfied but the s 84 target technically satisfied; and a defeated offence branch with no court disposition attached. For pre-2022 wording, use a distinct source-pinned model and never reuse the post-2022 expected result. Inputs must be labelled synthetic and must not name an actual person, diagnosis or judgment as a fact oracle.

## Acceptance, rejection and review record

Accept a **structure-only** pilot only if the reviewer can identify exact official source bytes and effective dates; approve each proposition's statutory versus interpretive status and version; identify the temporal-causal and wrongfulness grouping as a faithful *description*; set exact positive scope and exclusions; and approve restricted product wording. A candidate `ModelBundle-v1` must map every positive semantic ID to exact source-text bytes and report all material source limitations. An external `semantic_fidelity` or `source_fidelity` review assertion may then name the **exact bundle and scope digests**, exact reviewed IDs, methodology/version, limitations and outcome. Under `ModelBundle-v1`, such a record is **unsigned and descriptive**: it does not authenticate reviewer identity, qualification, official text or legal correctness. `scope_completeness` must not be inferred from an exclusion list; `jurisdictional_applicability` is not established by a date comparison.

Reject or defer executable doctrine if exact governing text or commencement cannot be checked; if the reviewer cannot approve the burden and temporal-causal grouping; if an anchor's statutory dependencies cannot be bounded; if a known later case/amendment changes a decisive premise; or if any output would be read as diagnosing, finding facts, assessing evidence, guilt, acquittal, fitness, custody or sentence. Reviewer disagreement should be recorded as competing assertions, not resolved by branch order or a technical overlap warning.

Prohibited product claims include “verified Singapore law”, “legally correct”, “authoritative model”, “complete defence engine”, “section 84 applies to this person”, “acquitted”, “unfit to plead”, “sentenced”, and “legal advice”. Yuho models registered legal propositions; it does not assess witnesses, diagnose mental conditions or determine facts. Even exact source hashes cannot establish that a retrieved version is applicable or that the model is legally complete.
