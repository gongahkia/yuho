# Assurance and limitations

Yuho is a formally founded research language, not a formally verified legal decision system.

| Claim area | Evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Core finite semantics | 79 registered Lean theorems, built with Lean 4.10.0 | Named definitions and theorems only; not the parser, compiler, filesystem resolver or diagrams |
| Haskell implementation | Independent retained conformance corpora of 95, 2,518 and 33 vectors | Bounded test evidence, not a general equivalence proof |
| Kernel compatibility | Eight protocol suites and 318 retained response-byte comparisons | Frozen technical behavior, not legal correctness |
| Corpus structure | Schema-checked 524-row saved Penal Code inventory | Structural indexing is not executable coverage or legal currency |
| Executable corpus | 26 families, 306 scenarios and 16 cases | Broad representative subset, expressly partial |
| Section 84 research | Preserved source mappings and scoped review records | Review is bounded to named artifacts and does not transfer to changed bytes |

The trusted computing base includes Lean and its kernel, GHC 9.8.4, the Haskell runtime and dependencies fixed by Cabal, the small conformance orchestration scripts, and the operating system and filesystem. Vector generation and byte comparison are test infrastructure; the Haskell and Lean evaluators compute results independently.

Yuho does not determine factual truth, evidence credibility, legal interpretation, commencement or currency, guilt, liability, conviction, acquittal, sentence, or criminal procedure. Its Singapore models are research artifacts that require professional review before any real-world use.
