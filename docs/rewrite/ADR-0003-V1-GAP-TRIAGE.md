# ADR-0003: v1 release gap triage

Status: accepted for Haskell Yuho Research Language v1.0.0.

## Decision

The release adds exactly three bounded representations:

1. finite normative positions (`required`, `prohibited`, `permitted`) with explicit applicability and priority;
2. the already versioned `PenaltyTerms-v1` death, life-imprisonment and bounded-caning forms at the Haskell `.yh` surface; and
3. author-defined, actor-attributed responsibility routes.

Norms and responsibility routes elaborate into the existing `TypedFiniteRules-v1` calculus. Rich sanctions lower to the existing `PenaltyTerms-v1` variant. No ninth kernel variant or protocol change is required.

## Corpus effect

The saved corpus identified 8 normative, 11 penalty-form and 15 participation-form primary gaps. The new syntax closes the language-level inability to state finite norms and routes, but it does not supply missing legal interpretation. Accordingly, the eight jurisdictional/normative rows and fifteen participation rows remain classified as gaps until a saved source supports an executable model. Section 147 is the one defensible corpus update: its saved imprisonment-and-caning form is now an authored candidate penalty, reducing the primary penalty-form count from 11 to 10. Sections 201 and 304 remain deferred because their tiered sanctions depend on predicate-offence or branch selection not responsibly encoded by the saved model.

## Deliberately unresolved

The 335-plus judicial-interpretation rows and 69 procedural rows are not compiler defects. Yuho does not automate judicial interpretation, evidence assessment, source currency, criminal procedure or sentencing discretion. Forfeiture, disqualification, contrary-to-duty obligations, remedies, obligation lifecycles and inferred actor responsibility remain optional research. They are not required for a coherent finite research release.

The release therefore retains its defensible 26 executable offence families, 55 Penal Code rows connected to executable models, 306 Singapore scenarios and 16 cases instead of padding those counts with shallow or unsupported encodings.
