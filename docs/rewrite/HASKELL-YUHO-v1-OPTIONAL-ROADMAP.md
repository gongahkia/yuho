# Optional work after Haskell Yuho Research Language v1.0

The v1 research language is coherent without another required feature milestone. Future work should be externally motivated and separately scoped:

- maintain compatibility with supported GHC and Lean toolchains;
- add reviewed corpus models when saved or newly commissioned legal research supports them;
- build editor conveniences, including a comment-preserving formatter and Haskell-authoritative language server;
- explore forfeiture, disqualification or procedural calculi only as separately specified research;
- apply the finite Core to another jurisdiction with its own source and review boundary.

Judicial interpretation, evidence assessment, guilt and sentencing remain outside the automated language rather than backlog items whose absence makes v1 incomplete.
