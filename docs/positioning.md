# Positioning

Yuho focuses on finite, inspectable criminal-law research models, exact-version local composition, technical-status execution, and source-visible explanations. It is comparable in kind to legal DSL and Rules as Code projects, but it does not claim their breadth, maturity or application domain.

- [L4](https://github.com/smucclaw/dsl) explores natural legal authoring and multiple downstream targets.
- [Catala](https://github.com/CatalaLang) gives legislative programs exception-aware executable structure.
- [OpenFisca](https://github.com/openfisca/openfisca-core) provides a mature platform for tax and benefit simulation.
- [Blawx](https://github.com/Lexpedite/blawx) emphasizes accessible visual rule authoring.
- [Docassemble](https://docassemble.org/) focuses on guided interviews and document automation.

Yuho’s narrower contribution is a Haskell-authoritative finite calculus with a machine-checked semantic boundary, bounded cross-implementation conformance, native semantic diagrams, and a structurally indexed but partially executable Singapore Penal Code research corpus. It is not a legal expert system or a substitute for legal interpretation.
