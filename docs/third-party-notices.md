# Distribution rights and third-party material

Software licensing and legal-source redistribution are separate decisions.
The owner approved Apache-2.0 for authorised original software, documentation,
examples and Lean code during this release-preparation task. The root LICENSE
contains the standard terms and NOTICE records that scope. Public repository
visibility alone is not permission, and this approval does not clear third-party
material for public redistribution.

Original implementation, original documentation/examples and original Lean code
can be covered only by a grant from their authorised rights holders. No ownership,
contributor assignment or licensing authority is inferred from Git names.

Statutory quotations, saved source exports/extracts, external data and third-party
assets are not automatically relicensed with project software. This includes
`library/penal_code/_raw/act.json`, saved `statute.yh` texts, research material
and statutory quotations embedded in examples, guides or fixtures. Source-intake
records retain limitations of their permission/authentication review. Separating
bulk research content from the SDK package does not establish permission for
every embedded quotation. Public redistribution requires a separate review or
exclusion of material that cannot be redistributed.

Cabal dependencies, GHC, Lean/Elan and test tools retain their own terms. Their
binaries and caches are not vendored in either source artifact. A future binary
distribution requires a dependency-license/notice review of its actual contents.
No new permission for third-party legal texts or dependencies is asserted here.

The [conditional legislation notice](legislation-notice.md) records the
Singapore Government copyright, AGC permission conditions, latest-version
direction, producer accuracy responsibility and non-endorsement statement.
It does not certify the retained material's acquisition or accuracy. The
[owner-authorisation record](owner-authorisation.md) retains the actual licence
selection from the supplied conversation, with its express scope limits.

The owner directly states that they drew the mascot and own its rights. That
statement resolves the missing creator evidence for the four
`assets/logo/yuho_mascot*` files. The owner subsequently explicitly approved
Apache-2.0 for all four files; the retained owner record includes that decision.
No third-party image-service origin or grant is invented.
The two indices `library/_index/sso_acts.json` and
`library/_index/ipc_bns_mapping.json` still have unresolved upstream permissions.
All six files are absent from the Cabal SDK archive but remain in the
repository, full bundle and reachable Git history.
Research judgment/speech quotations require their own assessment;
legislation permission is not an automatic grant for them. No deletion,
replacement, history rewrite or definitive exclusion boundary is authorised by
these notices. See [distribution boundaries](distribution-boundaries.md).
